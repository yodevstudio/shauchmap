ShauchMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What we collect

Location: Precise location is used only to find toilets near you. Never stored on our servers or shared.

Google Account: Name and email collected when you sign in, to track your contributions (ratings, toilets added).

Crash data: Anonymous crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics to improve stability.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not track you outside the app
  • We do not store your location history

Account deletion

Open ShauchMap app → Me tab → scroll down → tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, scout points, badges, and all associated data from our servers.
